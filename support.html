Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. The app has no sign-in and never asks for your name, email or phone number.

Where is my practice history stored?

In a local database on your device only. It is never uploaded. See the privacy policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes — everything except the store purchase flow works with no connection.

What does the subscription unlock?

Deeper analysis of your practice data. Recording drills, taking exams and browsing your full history stay free.

How do I get my data off the device?

Deleting the app deletes the local database, so keep that in mind before reinstalling.
