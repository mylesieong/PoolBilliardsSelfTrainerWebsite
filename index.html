What you get

Drills

A library that tells you why

Every drill carries a setup diagram, a skill focus — accuracy, cue-ball control, positioning — a difficulty, and an explanation of what it fixes in your game.

Sessions

Guided training sessions

Ready-made sessions from 5 to 60 minutes. The app leads you drill by drill, with timed breaks, so you never stand at the table deciding what comes next.

Exams

Self-assessment exams

Repeatable, standardised tests. Take one today, take the same one in a month, and see the difference in a number rather than a feeling.

Plans

Training plans

Set what you want to work on this week and how often. The home screen tells you what is due today.

Matches

Match tracking

Log real games and see whether the skills you have been drilling are showing up when it counts.

Progress

Evidence, not vibes

Success rates per drill, time spent per skill area, and trends over weeks — so you can tell improvement from a good day.

How it works

  1. Tell it where you are. Skill level, the thing that frustrates you most, and how long you have today.
  2. Practise what it proposes. A guided session, or pick a drill yourself and set your own reps and sets.
  3. Score each set as you go. Potted or missed, set by set — a few taps between racks.
  4. Test yourself periodically. Exams give you a stable benchmark that a single practice session can't.
  5. Read the trend. Weeks of records turn into a clear answer: is this working?
